# Third-party components

Sembrowse integrates, without vendoring, these MIT-licensed projects at the pinned revisions in `pyproject.toml`:

- [browser-use/jev-ultrafast](https://github.com/browser-use/jev-ultrafast), copyright its contributors.
- [TheoLeeCJ/SemIf-OpenJev](https://github.com/TheoLeeCJ/SemIf-OpenJev), copyright its contributors.

Installations retrieve their source through the pinned Git revisions. Model weights are neither bundled nor redistributed.
